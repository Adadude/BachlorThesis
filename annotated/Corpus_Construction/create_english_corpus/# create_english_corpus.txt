# English corpus creation

This script creates the final English-language Goodreads corpus from the six previously annotated Kafka review datasets.

For each dataset, it standardizes the language labels, records the source file and book title, and retains only reviews classified as English with a language-detection confidence score of at least 0.80. Reviews classified as non-English, unknown, or English with confidence below 0.80 are excluded.

The filtered reviews from all six Kafka works are then combined into a single corpus and saved as "kafka_english_corpus.csv".

The script also reports the total number of retained reviews and the number of reviews for each Kafka work.
